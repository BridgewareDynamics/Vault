NVIDIA Parakeet Automatic Speech Recognition Models
=====================================================

The Vault's transcription feature uses NVIDIA Parakeet speech recognition models,
including but not limited to:

  - nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2 (Parakeet TDT 0.6B V2)
  - nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 (Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3)
